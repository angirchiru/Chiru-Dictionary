Hishole Jihudi hai rohoi khat ruawm, khan Jesu na Jerusalem ah ruhawng joi. Jerusalem khan, Kengam Pakawt dieng ah awm, Hebrew chawng a Bethesda iti tuikhur khat ruawm. Khahai mun khan takshon, mikchaw, kekhawr, khan takthi miring hai na rurutung ua tui hawng rawnawk kha ngaklam a ruawm uh.

Kha mun khan kum shawm thumlieiriet natna niei miring khat ruawm. Jesu na aja a awm kha rumuk, khan khatikhan a awm kha ashawt nih joi, iti rujet le, ama dieng ruti, "Nangdam jawt maw?"

Anapa kha na ama dieng rumashang, "Oja, tui hawng rawnawk phakhan, tuikhur hin numuchum rang, mi niei miing. Khanikengta kashe phakhan, miring shik na kamatieng ruchummashathiei."

Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Thoi roh, nang jana kha loitana lawn rang roh." Relentak a ama kha rudam joi, khan ajana kha loi a rulawn joi. Makha nikho kha Sabbath nikho runih.

Khajarkhan imadampa dieng khan Jihudi hai kha na ruti uh, "Mahi Sabbath nikho inih, hi nikho hin nang jana nangichawi hi Dan nimak."

Khan Jihudi hai na Jesu anu kha ruthir ua ama kha thatna rang, tawk a ruawm uh. Ajarla hihaihi Sabbath nikho a iruthaw inih. Inia Jesu na amani dieng rumashang, "Kapa na tunshe hin chon thaw a awm, khan kieiawm chon kha kithaw inih."

Jesu na mashang a amani dieng ruti, "Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, Apa ithaw imuk kha niboia, Shapa kha na nining a ithaw ngam awm mak. Ajarla Apa ithaw hai kha le raang a Shapa na awm ithaw inih."

Ajarla Apa na athi hai kha imothoi a imiring kha ang a, Shapa na awm idit miring hai dieng ringna kha ipek inih.

"Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, tunitjoiawm kawchawng irangai a nojongtirpa kha takashawn ama kha na kumshawt ringna kha ieniei, khan chawngshatna shung khan va naw nih, inia athi ah put ah ringna khan rawthawt nih joi."

"Mahin shindawngna niei u naw roh, than shung ah awm kholok na ama hesho irangai ua vak shokna u rang a pha kha jong rangnih joi. Atha chon thaw hai na ringna rang a thoiretna tieng, khan ashie thaw hai na chawngshatna rang a thoiretna tieng she u rangnih."

"Kiei rangbak khan kieriei joiatu, kiriei kha adik nimak. Shik miring na kuchungchanga kungachang awm, khan kuchungchanga kungachang kha adik inih, iti kha kejet."

"Nangnitak na John dieng mi nangrutir uh, khan ama na adik khan kungruchangsha nih joi. John hi achong le avar thaomiei kha runih. Nangni na ama mieivar khan pha bawngte rawshawna rang, nangrumining uh."

"Kiei na John kungchangnanek a alien kungchangna kha kieniei, ajarla kapa na kiei dieng inepek chon hai kha rupummashakna rang, nurupek joi."

Nangni na Miejiek hai kha nangtawk uh, ajarla amani dieng kumshawt ringna kha awm, tia nangmining uh; khahaitak kha na inih kuchungchanga kungachang u kha. Inia ringna nangnieina u rang, kiei dieng va jawt u makche.
