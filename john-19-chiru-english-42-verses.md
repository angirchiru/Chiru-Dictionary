# John 19 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 14 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 1494+ verses - 1450+ CROSSED!

Khashole Pilate na Jesu kha ruchawi a rumejem joi. Khan ramiring hai na riling le iphan u renglukhum kha ama lu ah rurukhumashak uh. Khan ama kha ponshendup kha rurieshieimashak u joi. Khan ama dieng va ua ruti uh, "Lawm roh, Jihudi hai Reng hi!" Khan ama kha ruben uh.

Takthar a Pilate na vai vak moshok a amani dieng ruti, "Enro, ama shietna khatshe muk pe mi ing, iti nangjetna u rang, ama hi nangni dieng kivakshok puiret inih." Khashole Jesu na riling renglukhum kha rukhu a ponshendup rieshieilam a vak shok phakhan, Pilate na amani dieng ruti, "Miringpa kha hilah!"

Khashole thiempu rashang hai le riethiengmun shipai hai na rumuk u phakhan, ra ailam a ruti uh, "Ama kha cross ling ah that roh, cross ling ah that roh!" Pilate na amani dieng ruti, "Nangni na chawi u tana cross ling ah vak that u roh, ajarla ama shietna khatshe muk pe mi ing."

Jihudi hai na ama dieng rumashang uh, "Dan kieniei uh, nining a Pathien Shapa rurasha jarkhan, kadan u dungjuia a athingir rang a inih." Pilate na makha chawng kha rujet phakhan, ruchidet joi. Khan takthar a Praetorium shung khan rulut a Jesu dieng ruti, "Khoshekawt a jong maw nangnih?" Inia Jesu na ama dieng rumashanglai mak.

Khajarkhan Pilate na ama dieng ruti, "Kiei dieng chawngriei naw rawng maw? Nang hi cross ah thatna rang le moshokna rang, cham kieniei, iti hi jet makche maw?" Jesu na rumashang, "Chung ah put ah nang dieng inurupeksha nitnawkatu, kuchung a cham shukniei a runiei naw tinih."

Khan ah put ah Pilate na ama kha moshokna rang lam rutawk. Inia Jihudi hai na ra ailam a ruti uh, "Hi miring hi nangmoshok joiatu, Caesar lawmpa kha nilai makche. Tunita awm nining a reng rasha miring kha na Caesar re erpuina chawngiriei inih."

Pilate na hihai chawng hi rujet phakhan, ama na Jesu kha vak shok pui a Lunglamrakam iti u, inia Hebrew chawng a tu Gabbatha iti, chawngshat shutna khan rushuh joi. Khan Rakanna Rashamna Nikho nidan ruk dawr hawng nih joi. Khan Pilate na Jihudi hai dieng ruti, "Nang Reng u kha hilah!"

Amani na rura ai uh, "Chawite chawi rang roh, ama kha cross ling ah vak that rang roh." Pilate na amani dieng ruti, "Nang Reng u hi cross ling ah kithat rang a maw nih?" Thiempu rashang hai na rumashang, "Caesar niboia, reng niei u mi ing." Khashole cross ling ah thatna rang, ama kha amani dieng rupek joi.

Khan ama cross kha ruputlam a, Hebrew chawng a Golgotha iti Luru Mun khan, Jesu na ruhawng joi. Kha mun khan ama kha cross ling ah rumakhai u joi. Khan shir le shir ah miring shik idiawm makhai a Jesu kha alai ah rumakhaichen uh.

Pilate na khetmiejiek khat hi cross chung khan rumiejiek. Irumiejiek kha la: NAZARETH JESU HI JIHUDI HAI RENG INIH. Makha khetmiejiek kha Jihudi hai kar a tampui na rute uh. Ajarla Jesu cross ling ah irumakhaina u mun kha khopui le ruranai, khan miejiek kha Hebrew, Greek, khan Latin chawng a irumiejiek inih.

Khashole Jihudi hai thiempu ulien hai na Pilate dieng ruti uh, "Jihudi hai Reng, tia miejiek naw roh, inia Ama na Jihudi hai Reng kanih ruti, tia miejiek roh." Pilate na rumashang, "Kiemiejieksha kha miejieksho keng joi."

Ramiring hai na Jesu kha cross ling ah rumakhai u shole, ama ponledier kha ramiring rikip dieng tum khat a changna rang, tum mili a rushem uh. Khan takdep kha awm ruloi pe uh, inia takdep kha ichi boi, chung ah put ah rotho, inih.

Khan unu, unu naipangnu, Clopas inkonu Mary, khan Mary Magdalene, amani na Jesu cross diengtak khan ruding uh. Khashole Jesu na unu le numipok akhaichawi kha rumuk phakhan, unu dieng ruti, "Nupang, nang shapa kha en roh!" Khashole akhaichawi dieng khan ruti, "Nangnu kha en roh!" Makha pha ah put khan akhaichawi kha na ama kha i intak ah ruchawi joi.

Mahi shole, Jesu na ih kholok hawng mawtawr joi, iti rujet le, Miejiek kha hawng rupumna rang, "Kutuira," ruti. Kha mun khan ju athur shipmer be khat ruawm. Khajarkhan spongos kha ju athur khan rumechemen ua hyssop bak ah aje ua, abai ah ruthong pe uh. Jesu na ju athur kha rumechep shole, ruti, "Mawtawr joi." Khashole ulu mukun a aratha kha rupek joi.

Rashamna Nikho nit jarkhan, Jihudi hai na amani rok hai kha Sabbath shung a rutetna boi rang (ajarla makha Sabbath kha nikholien runih), amani keranga hai kha khiek pe a amani rok hai kha chawina rang, Pilate dieng vak nik uh. Khashole ramiring hai na va ua akhat changna le khan ama le cross ling ah imakhaishom shik khat ke hai kha vak khiek pe uh.

Inia Jesu dieng vat phakhan, ruthisha a ivakmuk u le roroia eke hai kha rukhiek pe u lai mak. Khania ramiring hai kar a akhat na shiei le edep ah vak shun a thishen le tui vak shokrelen joi. Khan amupa kha na kungruchang. Khan ama kungchangna kha adik inih.

Hihai ashok hi Miejiek kha hawng rupumna rang a inih: "Uru khatshe khiek pe naw nih." Khan Miejiek mun shik khat na ieriei, "Iruvit u ama kha en u rangnih."

Hihaihi shole, Jihudi hai kha chit jar a muruk a Jesu khaichawi ruchang, Arimathea Joseph na Jesu rok kha loina rang, Pilate dieng vak nik. Pilate na makha rujawt pe. Khashole rushe a Jesu rok kha vak loi joi.

Khan arapalai a jan a Jesu dieng rushe Nicodemus na awm myrrh le aloe reche litre rajakhat dawr hi vak chawi. Khashole amani na Jesu rok kha ruloi ua Jihudi hai khumna shedan dungjuia ponramasha le manamhoi rieipartui le rujo u joi.

Khan cross ling ah ithatna u mun khan rohon khat ruawm, khan makha rohon shung khan tute na ijana boi thankhur athar khat ruawm. Jihudi hai Rashamna Nikho nit jar a le khan than kha iedieng ah ruawm jarkhan, Jesu kha makha mun khan rumaja u joi.
