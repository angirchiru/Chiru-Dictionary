# Luke 17 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 13 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 315+ verses!

Khan Jesu na khaichawi hai dieng ruriei, "Keruthuina hai ajong boi rang a hi nithieilai naw nih, inia omojong ama rang khan, idawr a khawk maw nih naw! Riring u roh. Nang uliennai kha na munu thaw joiatu, rawkawk roh, khan umulung lek joiatu, rangaidam pe roh."

Khan tirtawn hai kha na Pumi dieng ruti uh, "Katakashawn u hi namarat pe shishit u roh." Pumi na ruriei, "Nangni na anchamru tiettak a awm takashawn kha nangniei uatu, mahi mana thingkung dieng hin 'Nang rawphawi tana tuikhanglien shung khawn vak mawng roh,' nangti u sheawm ama na anarangai pe rangnih."

Khan hitihin ruawm, Jesu na Jerusalem ah rushena khan Samaria le Galilee kha phaw a rupang. Khan ama na kho khat ah rulut phakhan, raithawnaw tawng apa shawm na rurumuk pui uh. Amani na ralaviei a ruding ua he rashangtak a ruti uh, "Aw Pumpa Jesu, nang roihoina kha kieini dieng natetna u roh!"
