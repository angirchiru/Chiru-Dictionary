Chiru Matthew 7:
Nang chung ua chawngshatna boi rang, mi chung a chawngshat u naw roh. 2 Ajarla mi chung a chawngnangishattak u kha na nang chung ua chawngshat rangnih. Nangni na dannangilak u kha na nangni dieng dannalak rangnih. 3 Ijar a nang mik ah awm thinglong kha tinta boi a, nang uliennai mik ah thinglek kha nangien maw nih? 4 Ninawkle, enro, thinglong kha nang mik ah awm a la, itia maw nang uliennai dieng 'Nang mik ah awm thinglek kha napai pe rawng jawt roh,' tia riei rawng? 5 Aw thanunchan thaw miring! Nang mik ah awm thinglong kha paibak roh, khashole nang uliennai mik ah put ah thinglek kha paina rang, malangsheng a nangmuk rangnih. 6 Riethieng nieinak kha ui hai dieng peh u naw roh, khan rangkachak hai kha vawk hai maton ah vawr pe u naw roh. Khanitatu amani chung khan eke le achongna rangnih, khan vak hiei ning, tia leklek a nether u rangnih.

Ani hai rang Mukna - Luk 11:9-13
7 Nih u roh, nangni dieng nepek rangnih. Tawk u roh, nangmuk u rangnih. Tawh u roh, nangni rang nawmawlawng pe rangnih. 8 Ajarla anih miring rikip na umuk, atawk ama kha na aman, khan atawh ama rang khan mawlawng pe. 9 Ninawkle, nang kar ua tukhat na ashapa na tan nik phakhan, ama dieng lung peh nih maw? 10 Ama na nga nik le ama dieng moro peh nih maw? 11 Mi shie nangnih uaawm, nang nai hai dieng khutchawi atha hai kha pekna rang, nangjet uala, ravan a awm nangpa u na, ama dieng a anih hai dieng khan khutchawi atha hai kha idawrshe maw arawp a peh nih? 12 Khajarkhan irikip a nangni dieng nangimawthaw jawt u kha thawna rang, amani dieng thaw u roh. Ajarla mahi Dan le Thiempulien hai na imiejiek kha inih.

Rebe le Rajai Pakawt
13 Pakawt rebe khan lut u roh, ajarla rajai pakawt le lamlien kha na thangna kha akhin, khan makhan a alut miring hai kha awrawp. 14 Ajarla ringna akhin pakawt kha rebe, lam kha matat, khan makha amuh hai kha awtawm.

Thingkung Khat le Ara
15 Thiempulien jok hai ah put ah riring u roh. Amani na kengam rielien thaw a va uh, inia amani shung a tu melang arang hai kha nih uh. 16 Amani ara hai khan nangjet u rangnih. Miring hai na riling kung ah put ah drakha, ninawkle thieimu kung ah put ah thieichang rawkhawm u maw? 17 Kha ang khan thingkung atha rikip na ara atha moshok, inia thingkung ashie na ara ashie moshok. 18 Thingkung atha na ara ashie moshok mak, khan thingkung ashie na ara atha moshok mak. 19 Ara atha moshok boi thingkung rikip kha tuk a miei ah apai. 20 Khatikhan amani ara khan nangjet u rangnih. 21 Kiei dieng 'Pumi, Pumi,' ati mi rikip na ravan rengchangna khan alut rang a nimak. Khania ravan a awm kapa mulungmining athaw ama kha na alut rang a inih. 22 Makha Nikho khan mi tampui na 'Pumi, Pumi, nang riming a chawng kirophong boi u, nang riming a raikho hai kinawt boi u, khan nang riming a ranakniei chon tampui kithaw boi u maw nih,' ti u rangnih. 23 Khan amani dieng 'Danishawnboi a chon thaw miring hai! Kiei na nangni kha shukjet a nejet u miing, kiei ah put ah she u rang roh,' tia korophong pe u rangnih.

Ashing le Amawng Inthaw hai - Luk 6:47-49
24 Khajarkhan tunitaawm kawchawng hai hi irangai a athaw miring hai kha lungpum chung ah inthaw miring ashing kha le raangmashak rang kanih. 25 Khan ro jong shur, tui hawng lien, khan rithi na ajap, khan amani na in kha ejem, inia pur mak, ajarla in kha lungpum chung ah iphun inih. 26 Tunitaawm kawchawng hai hi rangai a athaw boi rikip kha nge chung ah inthaw mimawng kha le raangmashak rang kanih. 27 Ro jong shur, tui hawng lien, rithi na ajap, khan hihaihi na in kha ejem, khan shietak a upur. 28 Khan hitihin ruawm, Jesu na hihai chawng hi ruriei shole, ama miruchukna khan mipui kha shinrudawng pe uh. 29 Ajarla danoja hai kha ang boi a, chamnieitak khat a amani kha iruruchuk inih

English Matthew 7
Judge not, that you be not judged... Why do you look at the speck in your brother's eye, but do not consider the plank in your own eye?... Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you... Enter by the narrow gate... You will know them by their fruits... whoever hears these sayings of Mine, and does them, I will liken him to a wise man who built his house on the rock...
