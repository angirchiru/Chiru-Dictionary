Chiru Matthew 6:22-34:
Nang niei bawmna mun khan nang mulungawm awm rangnih. 22 "Mik hi pum mieivar kha inih. Khajarkhan nang mik kha tha joiatu, nang pumplang kha mieivar kha na ship rangnih. 23 Inia nang mik kha shie joiatu, nang pumplang kha ajing na ship rangnih. Khajarkhan nang dieng a awm mieivar kha ajing nitjoiala, ajing kha idawr a lien maw nih! 24 Tute na pumi di chon thaw ngam naw nih, ajarla akhat kha mukmak a shik khat kha numpok rangnih. Ninawkle, akhat kha pawm a shik khat kha aheng rangnih. Pathien le nieilelung dire chon thaw ngam u naw tinih.

Mulung Mawshaw naw roh - Luk 12:22-31
25 Hijarhin a nangni dieng kiriei inih, imaw nangishak u rang ninawkle imaw nanginek u rang, tia nang ringkho u rang, imaw nangirieshiei u rang, tia nang pum u rang mulung mawshaw u naw roh. Nekleshaknek a ringkho, khan ponlediernek a nang pum u kha manludet nimak maw? 26 Ravan a va hai khi en u roh, vawrawm vawr u mak, atawm at u mak, khan achu aawm bawm u mak; inia ravan a awm nangpa u na amani kha ivai inih. Amaninek a manlu nih u makche maw? 27 Khan nang kar ua, tu na maw lungkhamna niei a atak chung ah rajang khat abo ngam? 28 Khan ponledier rang ijar a lungkhamna nanginiei u maw nih? Ram ah tuirieipar hai ringdan khaw tinta u roh. Chonawm thaw u mak, khan patawm kher u mak. 29 Inia nangni dieng kiriei inih, makhala Solomonshe na ama lurna kholok le hihaihi kar a akhat ang a rielien ngam mak. 30 Von mawng a jing tieng ratapkung ah ipai, ram ah champi hai khaw, Pathien na khatikhan rielien joiala, Aw ashin a takashawn miring hai, Pathien na nangni kha thadet a inierielien boi u rang a maw nih? 31 Khajarkhan, 'Imaw kenek rang'? ninawkle, 'Imaw kashak rang'? ninawkle, 'Imaw kierieshiei rang'? tia lungkhamna niei u naw roh. 32 Ajarla Phungshik hai na hihai rangbak hin rieirit a atawk uh. Ravan a nangpa u na hihaihi nangrangai u, iti kha ejet. 33 Inia Pathien rengchangna le ama dikna kha masha a tawk u roh, khan hihai kholok hi nangni dieng nobo pe u rangnih. 34 Khajarkhan jing a rang khan lungkhamna niei u naw roh. Ajarla jing kha na nining a lungkhamna kha niei rangnih. Nikho rikip na shawna kha rutukthep a iniei inih.

English Matthew 6:22-34
The lamp of the body is the eye... No one can serve two masters... do not worry about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink... Look at the birds of the air... Consider the lilies of the field... Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added to you. Therefore do not worry about tomorrow.
