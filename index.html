Matthew 4 - Wilderness & Galilee - NEW!
Chiru Matthew 4:
Khashole Jesu kha diabol na lemna rang, Ratha na ramthim ah ruchawi joi. 2 Khan ama na ni shawm mili jan shawm mili chamrarudoi shole, vawnrucham pe joi. 3 Makhan milempa kha na ava a ama dieng ruti, "Pathien Shapa kha nangnih a tu, hihai lung hi tan rachangna rang, chawng peh roh." 4 Inia mashang a ama na ruti, "Hitihin miejiek, 'Miring na nekleshak ranga kha le ring naw nih, inia Pathien bai ah put ah vak shok chawng rikip kha le aring rang a inih.' " 5 Khashole diabol na khopui riethieng khan ruchawi a riethiengmun moidawn khan rumiding. 6 Khan ama dieng ruti, "Pathien Shapa kha nangnih a tu, nang nining a chawm roh, ajarla hitihin miejiek, 'Pathien na ama ravantir hai kha nang chungchanga chawng pek rangnih,' khan 'Nangke kha lung le rawtawna boi rang, amani khut le nurudawm u rangnih.' " 7 Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Hitihin miejiek a awmret, 'Nang PUMI nang Pathien kha nangilem boi rang a inih.' " 8 Takthar a diabol na ama kha lawn rashangtak ah ruchawiret a manmieshie rengchangna kholok le amani lurna hai kha rumumuk. 9 Khan Jesu dieng ruti, "Kiei hi chubainangnabawk a tu, hihai kholok hi nepek rang kanih." 10 Khashole Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Kiei ah put ah rechen roh, Aw Satan! Ajarla hitihin miejiek a awm, 'Nang PUMI nang Pathien kha chubainangibawk rang a inih, khan ama ranga kha chon nangithaw pe rang a inih.' " 11 Khashole diabol na ama kha rumathan joi. Enro, ravantir hai na jong ua ama chon kha jong thaw pe u joi.

Jesu na Chawngshem Iputna
12 Khan John kha lungroko shung a rutet joi, iti kha Jesu na rujet le, Galilee tieng rushe joi. 13 Khan Nazareth kha rumathan shole, ava a Capernaum ah ruawm joi. Makha Zebulun le Naphtali ram ah thampui kawriei ah awm inih. 14 Makha thiempulien Isaiah dieng irurieina kha hawng rupumna rang a inih: 15 "Zebulun le Naphtali ram, thampui kawriei ah le Jordan vara ah awm, Phungshik hai awmna Galilee a. 16 Ajing a awm miring kha na mieivar lientak khat muk joi. Khan kha ram le thitna rilip a awm miring amani dieng khan mieivar kha na hawng eh joi." 17 Khan ah put khan Jesu na chawngshem ruput a ruriei, "Nang mulung lek u roh, ajarla ravan rengchangna kha jong nai joi."

Akhaichawi hai Ikoina
18 Khan Galilee Thampui pang kha rujotlai khan, poro di, Peter iti Simon le anaipangpa Andrew, na thampui chung ah len iruvawr u kha vak muk, ajarla amani kha ngashur miring runih uh. 19 Khan adin u dieng ruti, "Kanu nujui u roh, kiei na nangni kha mishur miring niminit u rang kanih." 20 Khan relentak a len hai kha mak ua Jesu anu kha rujui u joi. 21 Khan ah put ah rushe a ulienpa le naipangpa shik idi, Zebedee shapa James le anaipangpa John kha rokong shung ah vak muk uh. Amani kha apa u Zebedee le rokong shung ah len hai kha iphawt a ruawm u inih, khan amani kha rukoi uh. 22 Khan relentak a rokong le apa u kha mak ua Jesu anu kha rujui u joi.

Galilee ah Jesu Chonthaw
23 Jesu na Galilee kholok khan rushe a amani synagogue hai khan mi ruruchuk. Khan rengchangna athachawng kha rurophong. Ama na rai rikip le miring hai kar a taksha natna kholok kha rumadam pe joi. 24 Khan Syria kholok shung khan iriming rushe joi. Khan ana hai kholok kha ama dieng ruchawi uh. Amani kha rai shikshik natna na imawshaw, raikho na ichutna, pingbawprai tawng miring hai le takthi miring hai kha runih uh. Jesu na amani kha rumadam uh. 25 Khan Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, khan Jordan vara ah put ah mipui na ama anu kha rujui u joi.

English Matthew 4
Then Jesus was led up by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil. And when He had fasted forty days and forty nights, afterward He was hungry... "Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God."... "You shall not tempt the Lord your God."... "You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him only you shall serve."... Then Jesus began to preach and to say, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand."... "Follow Me, and I will make you fishers of men."... And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all kinds of sickness... Great multitudes followed Him.
Matthew 3 - John Baptisma
Chiru: Khalaikhan baptisma apeh John na Judea ramthim khan rular a chawng rushem: "Nang mulung lek u roh, ajarla ravan rengchangna kha jong nai joi!"... John na ruti "Mahi kashapa numpoktak kha inih, ama chung a ningnitna kha kieniei joi."

English Matthew 3: In those days John the Baptist came preaching in the wilderness of Judea, and saying, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!"... "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased."
Matthew 2 - Complete
Chiru Matthew 2: Herod na reng ruchanglai, Judea ah awm Bethlehem khan Jesu rushok shole... Khan amani na rumathan u shole, Pumi ravantir na Joseph dieng ramang a jong lar a ruti, "Thoi rang roh, Naitepa le unu kha chawi tana Egypt ah tan rang roh."... Khan ama na ava a Nazareth iti kho khan vak awm joi.

English Matthew 2: Now after Jesus was born in Bethlehem... flee to Egypt... He shall be called a Nazarene.
