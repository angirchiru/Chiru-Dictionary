Chiru Matthew 5:39-48:
Nangni dieng kieriei, mi shie kha rodoi pui u naw roh, inia tukhat na nang ngem chang tieng naben phakhan, shir khat khaawm daw pe roh. 40 Tukhat na nang kotai kha loina rang nemeher joiatu, nang pon inoloi pe rang a khaawm jawt roh. 41 Tukhat na nang kha majin khat shena rang neep joiatu, majin di she pui roh. 42 Nang dieng nanih ama dieng khan peh roh. Nang dieng naphuk jawt miring ah put ah nang mai miehiei naw roh.

Rahapui hai kha Numpokna
43 "Nang ringchangpui kha numnangipok rang a inih, khan nang rahapui kha nangimukmak rang a inih," iti kha nangrujet uh. 44 Inia nangni dieng kieriei, nang rahapui hai kha numpoh u roh. Pumnaboh hai kha rahvan peh u roh. Namumah hai dieng atha thaw u roh. Mawchawlaw a norotokna le epshawna napeh amani rang nik pe u roh. 45 Hihaihi nangthaw uatu, ravan a awm nangpa u shapa hai kha nangchang u rangnih. Ajarla atha ashie hai rang ama ni imoshok pe inih. Midik midik boi hai rang ro imushur pe inih. 46 Ajarla numnapoh hai kha numnangpok a la, ih lawh kha maw niei rawng? Khaichana rawkhawm hai naawm makha ithaw u nimak maw? 47 Khan nang uliennai ranga kha chubai nangthaw pui a la, shik hainek a imaw nangithawdet awm? Phungshik hai naawm ithaw u nimak maw? 48 Khajarkhan ravan a awm nangpa u urupum kha ang a, nangniawm rupum u roh

English Matthew 5:39-48
You have heard that it was said, 'An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.' But I tell you not to resist an evil person. But whoever slaps you on your right cheek, turn the other to him also... Give to him who asks you... You have heard that it was said, 'You shall love your neighbor and hate your enemy.' But I say to you, love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who spitefully use you and persecute you... Therefore you shall be perfect, just as your Father in heaven is perfect.
