Chiru:
Khalaikhan baptisma apeh John na Judea ramthim khan rular a chawng rushem: “Nang mulung lek u roh, ajarla ravan rengchangna kha jong nai joi!”

3 Ajarla mahi miringpa chungchang hin thiempulien Isaiah dieng a irurieina inih. Ama na ruti, “Ramthim ah hesho khat na raai, ‘PUMI lam kha kon pe roh. Ama lam hai kha moton pe roh.’ ”

4 Makha Johntak na sharingshiei sham kha rurieshiei, mevun kawngkhit kha rukhit. Ama nekleshak kha khaishawr hai le ram khoining runih. 5 Khashole Jerusalem, Judea le Jordan na iroko ram ah awm miring kholok na ama dieng rushe uh. 6 Khan amani munu hai kha ruruphong ua Jordan khan ama na baptisma rupek uh.

7 Inia Pharisee hai le Sadducee hai na ama baptisma loina mun ah ava kha rumuk phakhan, amani dieng ruti, “Aw moro chicha hai! Jongshok rang a lunghangna ah put ah atan rang, tu na maw nangni kha nierieimasha u joi? 8 Khajarkhan mulung lekna rutuk ara hai kha mupung u roh. 9 ‘Abraham kha kapa u khat a kieniei uh,’ tia mining u naw roh. Ajarla hihai lung ah put ah Abraham rang Pathien na nai hai kha mothoi ngam, tia nangni dieng kinieriei u inih.

10 Tunawm thing hai rujung khan riei kha rutung a awm joi. Khajarkhan atha ara moshok boi thing rikip kha tuk a miei ah apai rangnih. 11 “Nang mulung nangilek u le roroia, kiei na nangni kha tui a baptisma kenepek uh, inia kanuk a ava rang a pa kha kieinek a ranak nieidet. Ama kethawp hai arak rang khan kiei rutuk miing. Ama na nangni kha Ratha Riethieng le miei a baptisma nepek u rangnih. 12 Jamja kha ukhut le arak joi. Ama na pher kha machai rangnih joi. Khan ama gehu kha achu khan rawkhawm rangnih joi, inia irishi hai kha chiemtiknieiboi miei le ha rangnih joi.”

John na Baptisma Ipekna
13 Khashole John na baptisma moloina rang, Jesu na Galilee ah put ah Jordan khan rushe joi. 14 Inia John na rumakha a ruti, “Nang na kiei hi baptisma ninomoloi rang a hi kirangai inih. Inia iloia kiei dieng ava maw nangnih?” 15 Inia Jesu na mashang a ama dieng ruti, “Tun a hin tu hi nih rosho, ajarla dikna kholok hitihin a rupummashak rang a hi kieini rang rutukthep inih.” Makhan John na rujawt pe joi. 16 Khan baptisma rupek shole, Jesu kha relentak a tui ah put ah hawng thoi a enro, ravan kha rumawlawng pe joi. Khan Pathien Ratha na vashu ang a jong chum a ama dieng jong chuh kha rumuk. 17 Khan enro, ravan ah put ah he khat na ruti, “Mahi kashapa numpoktak kha inih, ama chung a ningnitna kha kieniei joi.

English Matthew 3 - Repent, Kingdom of Heaven
In those days John the Baptist came preaching in the wilderness of Judea, and saying, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” For this is he who was spoken of by the prophet Isaiah, saying: “The voice of one crying in the wilderness: ‘Prepare the way of the Lord; Make His paths straight.’ ” Now John himself was clothed in camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist; and his food was locusts and wild honey. Then Jerusalem, all Judea, and all the region around the Jordan went out to him and were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins... Then Jesus came from Galilee to John at the Jordan to be baptized... a voice came from heaven, saying, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”
