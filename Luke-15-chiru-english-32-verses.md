## Chiru (First Digital Corpus)

Khan khaichana rawkhawm hai kholok le munu miring hai kholok na ama chawngriei rangai rang, hawng ranai pui uh. Khan Pharisee hai le danoja hai na mieshietlam a ruti uh, "Hi miring hi na munu miring hai kha awphawm a amani le ashak." 

Ama na mahi rangshim hi riei pe a, amani dieng ruti, "Nangni kar a tu khat na kengam rajakhat iniei kar khan akhat thang pe joi. Ama na shawmkolieiko kha ramthim ah mak a athang khat kha muk masheng, atawk she naw nih maw? Ama na muk phakhan, iring ah mushut a ama na ningai pui."

Ninawkle, nupang khat na drachma shawm ieniei, ama na drachma khat mathang joiatu, thaomiei muchuk ning, tia in kha phiet rangnih. Ama na muk phakhan, awlawm hai le ringchangpui hai kha rawkhawm a 'Kimathang drachma kha kumuk joi jarkhan, ningnavakngai pui u roh,' iti.

Khashole Jesu na ruriei, "Miring khat na shapa di runiei. Naipanglampa kha na apa dieng ruti, 'Kapa, kitumphawm nieinak kha nepeh rang roh.' Apa na nieilelung kha adin u dieng rushem pe joi."

"Khan ashapa kha na ama dieng ruti, 'Kapa, ravan chung a le nang maikung a munu kawthaw joi. Nang shapa khat, tia koi rang hi kiei rutuklai mi ing.'"

"Khan apa kha na oshok hai dieng ruti, 'Relentak a pon athaving kha vak chawi u tana ama hi vak rieshieimashak u roh, khan ukhut hin shumkieviek vak mietieng u roh; khan eke hai hin kekok vak mawrawn pe u roh."
