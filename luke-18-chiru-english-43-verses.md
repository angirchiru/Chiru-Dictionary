# Luke 18 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 13 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 358+ verses!

Khashole ningthingkip mulungrakai boi a nikna rang, Jesu na amani dieng rangshim ruriei pe. Ama na ruriei, "Khopui khat hin Pathien chih boi le mi ishawn boi athaw chawngshat miring khat ruawm. Khan makha khopui khan mieirawng khat ruawm; mieirawng kha na ama dieng ava a ruti, 'Nere erpui ah put ah kiei hi nimidik roh.'"

Khan Pumi na ruti, "Midik boi chawngshatpa iriei kha rangai u roh. Ama na amani le shawttak doi pui joiawm, shun le jan a ra ai imadansha hai kha Pathien na midik boi a maw tet nih?"

Miring pasha di hi na riethiengmun ah anih rushe uh. Akhat kha Pharisee inih, khan akhat kha khaichana rawkhawm miring inih.

Khan hitihin ruawm, ama na Jericho ivakranai pui le roroia, mikchaw miring khat na lamkaw khan khutdawlam a ruawm. Ama na ra ailam a ruti, "David Shapa Jesu, nang roihoina kha kiei dieng hin natetna roh!"

Khan Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Muh rang roh, nang takashawn kha na namadam joi." Khan relentak a imik kha hawng var pe joi, khan Pathien kha mapaklam a anu rujui joi.
