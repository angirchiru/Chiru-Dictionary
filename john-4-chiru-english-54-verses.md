Khashole Jesu na Johnnek a arawpdet khaichawi kon a baptisma pek joi, tia Pharisee hai na nokor a irujet u kha Pumi na rujet phakhan, ama na Judea kha mathan a Galilee ah rusheret joi. Khan Samaria kha phaw a ama ashe rang a inih.

Khajarkhan Jacob na ashapa Joseph dieng a irupek ram dieng ah awm, Sychar iti Samaria kho khan rushe joi. Makhan Jacob tuikhur ruawm. Apha kha nidan ruk dawr runih.

Samaria ah put ah nupang khat hi tuilah ava. Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Nek rang tui nepeh roh." Khashole Samaria nupangnu kha na ama dieng ruti, "Itia Jihudi miring nang na Samaria nupang kiei dieng hin nek rang tui nangininik maw nih?" Ajarla Jihudi miring hai na Samaria miring hai le rawlawpngai mak.

Jesu na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Nang na Pathien khutchawi le nang dieng 'Nek rang tui nepeh roh,' tia ananipa kha tumaw nih, iti kha nangrujet joiatu, ama dieng nangrunik a tuiring kha nurupekawm jawt joi."

Jesu na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Tunita awm mahi tui aneh miring kha na tuirana nieiret rangnih. Inia kiei na kipek rang a tui aneh ama kha na tu, tuirana shukniei a nieilai naw nih. Khania kipek rang a tui hi na ama dieng kumshawt ringna rang a aput jawnjai tuinar kha chang pe rangnih."

Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "She tana nang inkopa kha vak koi roh, khan hi mun hin va roh." Nupangnu kha na mashang a ruti, "Inkopa niei miing." Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Inkopa rahnga nangruniei joi, khania nanginiei khaw tu nang inkopa nimak, makha nangiti kha adik inih."

Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Aw nupang, takanawshawn roh, ajarla Apa kha hi lawn hinawm, khan Jerusalem a awm biekna boi rang a pha kha jong nai joi. Pathien hi Ratha inih, khan ama abieh miring rikip na ratha a le adik a ibiekngir rang a inih."

Nupangnu kha na ama dieng ruti, "Messiah kha jong rangnih, iti kha kejet." Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Ama kha nang dieng a chawng ariei, kiei hi kanih."

Khashole nupangnu kha na ama tuibe kha rumak a kho khan rushe joi. Khan miring hai dieng vak riei, "Miring khat na kiruthawsha chon kholok kiei dieng nieriei pe joi. Haro, vak en u rang roh, ama hi Christa kha ninih maw?"

Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Kiniei nekleshak hi tu nojongtirpa mulungmining athaw le ama chon rupummashak hi inih. Enro, nang ringrakanglam a en u roh, ajarla amani khaw rawkhawm rang, minsho joi!"

"Ama na kiruthawsha chon kholok kiei dieng nururiei," tia nupangnu kungchangna jar a makha kho ah awm Samaria miring tampui na ama kha takarushawn uh.

Khashole Jesu na tui kha ju imachangna mun, Galilee ah awm Cana kho khan varet. Capernaum khan, ashapa dam pe boi, rengmun ah chonpo miring khat ruawm.

Jesu na Judea ah put ah Galilee ah va joi, iti rujet phakhan, ashapa kha madamnathiei rang, nojong pe roh, tia runik.

Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "She rang roh, nang shapa kha dam joi." Miringpa kha na Jesu na iruti kha takashawn a rushe joi. Khajarkhan apa kha na Jesu na ama dieng "Nang shapa kha dam joi," irutina pha kha inih, iti kha rujet.
