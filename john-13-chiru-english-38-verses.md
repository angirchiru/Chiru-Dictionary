# John 13 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 14 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 1295+ verses - 1250+ CROSSED!

Rakanna rohoi malatak khan Jesu na manmieshie hi mathan a apa dieng hawngna rang a pha kha jong joi, iti kha rujet. Manmieshie ah awm ama tatak hai kha rupumping a numrupok joi. Tun janbu runek u lai khan diabol na Simon shapa Judas Iscariot mulung shung a ama kha jorna rang, mulungmining rupekmasha joi.

Apa na ama khut chung a ih kholok irupek, Pathien ah put ah ajong, khan Pathien dieng ahawng rang a kha Jesu na rujet. Janbu nekna ah put ah ama na ruthoi a opon rieshiei kha atet a pontete kha loi a kawng ah ruverep joi. Khan thingkheng ah tui kha rushung a akhaichawi hai ke rushuk ruput joi. Khan awkawng a iruverep pontete kha le runo pe joi.

Simon Peter dieng vat phakhan, Peter na Jesu dieng ruti, "Pumi, keke nanginurushuk pe rang a maw nih?" Jesu na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Nang dieng kithawtak hi tun hin jet naw tinih, inia nuk a nangjet rangnih." Peter na ama dieng ruti, "Keke hi shukrushuk a nurushuk pethiei naw tinih."

Jesu na ama dieng rumashang, "Nang ke kunurushuk pe nawkatu, nang hi kiei le tak khat changna kha niei makche." Simon Peter na ama dieng ruti, "Pumi, keke ranga hi niboia, kukhut hai le kulu hiawm nurushuk pe roh."

Amani ke hai kha rurushuk pe shole, opon rieshiei kha loi a tebol khan vak shuret a amani dieng ruti, "Nangni dieng a kithawsha hai kha nangjet u maw? Oja le Pumi, tia nangnokoi uh, makha adik inih, ajarla makha kiei kanih. Nang Pumi u le nang Oja u kiei na nang ke u hai kha kunurushuk pe u joiatu, nangniawm khatlekhat ke hai kha nangirushuk u rang a kha nangiton u inih."

Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, shok kha upuminek a liendet mak, khan itir ama kha atirpanek a liendet mak. Hihaihi nangjet ua nangthaw u joiatu, rahvanmuh miring kha nangnih u joi.

Hihaihi ruriei shole, Jesu na ratha a shawna runiei a rurophong, "Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, nang kar ua akhat na kiei hi nojor rangnih joi." Khajarkhan tu chungchanga iti maw nih, iti mulung rakhana le roroia akhaichawi hai kha na khatlekhat dieng rure en uh.

Jesu na numipok akhaichawi hai kar a akhat na ama kha rungai ir a ruawm. Khajarkhan Simon Peter na, tu chungchanga iriei maw nih, iti kha rawdawnna rang, ama dieng imik rumakhapna. Khan Jesu kha angairet a ama dieng ruti, "Pumi, tumaw nih?" Jesu na rumashang, "Khengkam ah kinawm tanlek kipek rang a pa hi inih."

Khajarkhan tan kha khengkam khan runawm a Simon shapa Judas Iscariot dieng rupek joi. Tanlek kha ruloi shole, Satan na ama dieng rulut joi. Khajarkhan Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Nangithaw rang a chon kha relentak a thaw rang roh."

Khajarkhan tanlek kha ruloi shole, nawtshun a rushe joi. Makha jan runih. Ama na rushe shole, Jesu na ruriei, "Tun Miring Shapa kha mulur joi, khan ama dieng Pathien na lurna niei joi."

"Naite hai, pha bawngte nangni le awm rang kanilai, khan kiei hi nangnatawk u rangnih. Khana Jihudi hai dieng kiruti kha ang a, 'Keshena rang a mun khan nangni va ngam u naw tinih.' Khajarkhan tun nangni dieng kiriei hi inih."

"Nangni dieng chawngpek thar kenepek uh, makhala nangni khatlekhat numropoh u roh. Khan kiei na nangni numkinopok u hi ang a, nangni khatlekhat numropoh u roh. Nangni na khatlekhat numnangropoh u joiatu, mi kholok na kakhaichawi hai kha nangnih uh, iti kha jet u rangnih."

Simon Peter na ama dieng ruti, "Pumi, khoshekawt ah she rang a maw nangnih?" Jesu na ama dieng rumashang, "Keshena rang a mun khan, tun nujui ngam naw tinih. Inia nuk a nangnujui ngam rangnih." Peter na ama dieng ruti, "Pumi, ithaw tun kinujui ngam boi rang a maw nih? Nang rangbak kiringkho pek rang kanih." Jesu na ama dieng rumashang, "Kiei rang nang ringkho peh rawng maw? Diktak a nang dieng kiriei inih, voithum nangrapha masheng arkhawng na khong naw nih."
