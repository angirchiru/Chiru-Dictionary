Khan hitihin ruawm, nikhat hin Jesu na riethiengmun ah miring hai kha ruruchuk a athachawng rushemlai khan, thiempu ulien hai le danoja hai na, ama kha rurodoi pui uh. Amani na riei a, ruti uh, "Ih cham nanginiei a hihaihi nangithaw maw nih?"

Khan ama na amani dieng mahi rangshim hi ariei ruput joi: "Miring khat na drakha rohon khat hi rurohon a loithaw miring hai dieng rupek joi. Khan ama na pha shawttak rang khan khomijin rushe joi."

Khan amani na rawdawn a ruti uh, "Oja, Caesar dieng khaichana pek hi Dan maw nih, ninawkle dan nih boi maw nih?" Khan Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Caesar tah hai kha Caesar dieng peh u roh, khan Pathien tah kha Pathien dieng peh u roh."

Khan thoiretna awm mak ati Sadducee eshen na ama kha vak jawng uh. Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Ama hi athi miring hai Pathien nimak a aring miring hai Pathien kha inih."
