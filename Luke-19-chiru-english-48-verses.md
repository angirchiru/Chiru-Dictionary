# Luke 19 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 13 Nov 2026

Khan Jesu na Jericho kha rulut a rupang joi. Khan enro, Zacchaeus iti miring khat ruawm. Ama kha khaichana rawkhawm ulienchang le mirawchawng khat runih. Ama na Jesu kha tumaw nih, iti kha rumuk jawt, inia dungtawi miring runit jarkhan, mipui kar khan ama na rumuh ngam mak. Khan ama na maton shawn a rutan a Jesu vak pangna rang a lam khan muknathiei rang, ama na thieipong kung khat ah ruka.

Khan Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Von nikho hin shandamna kha hi inshung hin jong joi. Ajarla ama hiawm Abraham shapa khat inih."

Khan Jesu na hihaihi ruriei shole, ama awm Jerusalem tieng rushe joi. Khan amani na Bethphage le Bethany vak shokdeng phakhan, akhaichawi hai pasha di kha tir a ruti.
