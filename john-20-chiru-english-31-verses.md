Hapta shung a akhat changna nikho varmuk a, Mary Magdalene na thanmun ah vat phakhan, than ah put ah lungpek rurawthawt kha vak muk. Khajarkhan vak tan a Simon Peter le Jesu na numipok akhaichawi shik khat dieng ava a amani dieng ruti, "Than shung ah put ah Pumi kha ruloi u joi, ama kha khoshekawt ah itet u maw nih joi, jet u lai mi ing."

Khashole Peter na khaichawi shik khat kha le rushe a than khan va u joi. Pasha di kha na rutanshom ua khaichawi shik khat kha na Peter kha rukhe a thanmun kha matieng rushok joi. Ama na butlam a ruen a ponramasha hai rapai kha vak muk, inia rulut maklai.

Khashole Simon Peter na ama kha rujui a than shung khan vak lut; khan ponramasha hai rapai kha vak muk. Khan lu ah ruawm pontete kha ponramasha hai kha le rurapai mak, inia shikjek a mun khat ah rurethep a ruawm. Khashole thanmun ah matieng rushok khaichawi shik kha na awm rulut joi, khan ama na rumuk a takarushawn joi.

Khajarkhan takthar a akhaichawi hai na amani inin ah rukir u joi. Inia than vai tieng khan Mary na ding a rie iekklam a ruchap. Achap le roroia than shung khan butlam a ruen. Khan ponchek rieshiei ravantir idi kha Jesu rok irena mun khan ulu tieng khat, khan eke tieng khat rushuh u kha rumuk.

Amani na ama dieng ruti uh, "Nupang, ijar a chap maw nangnih?" Amani dieng ruti, "Ajarla kupumi kha nuruchawi pe u joi, khoshekawt a inatet pe u maw nih joi, jetlai mi ing." Hihaihi ruti shole, anuk tieng vak hiei a Jesu ading kha vak muk, inia Jesu keng, iti rujet mak.

Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Nupang, ijar a chap maw nangnih? Tumaw nangitawk?" Rohon rung miring khat a rumining a ama dieng ruti, "Oja, ama kha ninuruchawi pe nitjoiatu, khoshekawt ah nitet maw nih, nieriei roh. Kiei na chawi rang kanih joi." Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Mary!" Ama na vak hiei a ama dieng ruti, "Rabboni!" (mahi Oja tina inih).

Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Numukui a awmlai naw roh, 'Kiei na kapa dieng hawng mi inglai. She tana kuliennai hai dieng vak riei rang roh. Kiei na kapa le nangpa u, kapathien le nang Pathien u dieng ahawng rang kanih joi.' " Mary Magdalene na rushe a Pumi kha umuk joi, khan ama dieng hihaihi riei joi, tia khaichawi hai dieng vak rophong joi.

Hapta shung a akhat changna nikho nitak khan akhaichawi hai na Jihudi hai kha ruchih ua, ruruchunna u in mawkawt kha rukhar uh. Jesu na hawng lut a amani kar khan hawng ding a ruriei, "Lungdamna kha nangni le awm rosho." Makha ruriei shole, amani dieng ukhut hai le edep kha rumumuk uh. Khajarkhan khaichawi hai kha na Pumi kha rumuk u phakhan, ningrungai pe uh.

Takthar a Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Lungdamna kha nangni dieng awm rosho. Kapa na inojongtir hi ang a, nangniawm kinitir uh." Mahi ruriei shole, amani dieng phuk thong pe a ruriei, "Ratha Riethieng kha tum u roh. Miring hai munu kha nangrangaidam pe u joiatu, irangaidam pe inih."

Inia Didymus iti u shawmlieidi kar a akhat Thomas kha Jesu na vatlai khan amani le ruawm mak. Khaichawi shik hai na ama dieng ruti uh, "Pumi kha kumuk u joi." Inia amani dieng ruti, "Ukhut ah hemthir shebe kha kumuk nawk a, hemthir shebe kha kukhut mieriei le kama nawk a, khan edep kha kukhut le kishit nawkatu, takashawn naw ning."

Ni riet shole, takthar a akhaichawi hai na inshung khan ruawm uh. Thomas awm amani le ruawm joi. Mawkawt kha rurakhar joiawm, Jesu kha hawng lut, khan amani kar a ding a ruriei, "Lungdamna kha nangni dieng awm rosho." Khashole Thomas dieng ruti, "Nang khut mieriei kha vak shawn tana vak ma roh, khan nang khut kha vak shawn tana kedep hi vak shih roh. Takashawn boi miring kha niboia, takashawn miring kha chang roh."

Thomas na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Kupumi kapathien." Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Thomas, nangnumuk joi jarkhan, takanangnawshawn joi. Amuh boi a takashawn miring hai kha rahvanmuh miring kha inih."

Khan mahi behangbuk ah imiejiek boi Jesu na akhaichawi hai dieng iruthaw jetna tampui awmlai. Khania Jesu hi Christa, Pathien Shapa kha inih, iti takanangshawnna u rang a irumiejiek inih. Makha, takanangshawn u phakhan, ama riming a ringna kha nangnieina u rang a inih.
