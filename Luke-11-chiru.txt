Chiru: Luke 11:1-54

[1] Khan hitihin ruawm, Jesu na mun `khat ah nikna runiei. Ama na rubang phakhan, akhaichawi hai kar a akhat na ama dieng ruti, "Pumi, John na akhaichawi hai iruchuk kha ang a, nikna dan nuruchuh u roh." 
[2] Khan ama na ruti, "Nangni na nangnik u phakhan, hitihin ti u roh: Ravan a awm kapa u, nang riming kha riethieng nih rosho, nang rengchangna kha jong rosho. Ravan a nang mulungmining nangithaw kha ang a, rieliei hinawm nih rosho. 
[3] Von a nekkhawp nekleshak kha nikip nepeh u roh. 
[4] Kielieishie ton miring rikip kha karangaidam u jarkhan, kumunu u hai hi narangaidam pe u roh. Lemna a nomocho u naw roh. Inia ashie ah put ah nakhek u roh." 
[5] Khan amani dieng ruriei, "Nang kar ua akhat na lawm `khat ieniei, khan ama na janchim a ama dieng she a iti, 'Kama, tan `thumbak nepeh roh. 
[6] Ajarla kawlawm `khat khomijin ah put ah jong tung a ama shak rang ih nieilai mi`ing.' 
[7] Khan ama na inshung ah put ah rumashang, 'Kiei hi nachawklai naw roh, tun mawkawt kha rakhar joi, khan kanai hai kha kiei le jakhang ah ja u joi; kiei na thoi a nepeh ngamlai naw ning.' 
[8] Kiei na nangni dieng kieriei, ama kha awlawm nitjoiawm, ruthoi pe mak, inia ringrawrat jarkhan, ama na thoi a irangai rikip kha pek rangnih. 
[9] "Khan kiei na nangni dieng kieriei, nih u roh, nangni dieng nepek rangnih. Tawk u roh, nangmuk u rangnih. Tawh u roh, nangni rang nawmawlawng pe rangnih. 
[10] Ajarla anih miring rikip na umuk, atawk ama kha na aman, khan atawh ama rang khan mawlawng pe. 
[11] "Nang kar ua apa `khat dieng shapa kha na tan nik `le, lung peh nih maw? Nga nik `le, kha ruhut a moro peh nih maw? 
[12] Khan arrutui nik `le, kha ruhut a aikoshangpa peh nih maw? 
[13] Khajarkhan, ashie nangnih u awm, nang nai hai dieng nieinak atha apeh kha nangthiei uala, ravan a awm nangpa u kha na anih miring hai dieng Ratha Riethieng kha idawr maw nepeh u nih!" 
[14] Khan ama na chawngthieiboi raikho `khat runawt. Raikho kha ama ah put ah rushok phakhan, chawngthieiboi miring kha ruchawng joi. Khan mipui kha shinrudawng pe uh. 
[15] Inia eshen na ruti uh, "Raikho hai reng Beelzebul ranak a raikho hai hi inawt inih." 
[16] Khan eshen na, enretena rang, ravan ah put ah jetna ama dieng runik uh. 
[17] Inia amani mulungmining kha rujet pe a amani dieng ruti, "Nining a reshen rengchangna rikip thangbieng rangnih, khan malema re`er in kha upur. 
[18] Khan Satan kha na nining a reshen joiala, iti a maw ama rengchagna kha ding nih? Ajarla Beelzebul ranak a raikho hai kha kinawt keng nangti uh. 
[19] Inia kiei na Beelzebul ranak a raikho hai kha kawnawt joiala, nang nai hai na tu ranak a maw amani kha nawt uh? Khajarkhan amani kha nang chawngshat hai kha nih u rangnih. 
[20] Inia Pathien khutlawi kha le raikho hai kha kawnawt joiatu, nang chung ua Pathien rengchangna kha jong shok joi." 
[21] Miratpa kha na mieriem le rashamjer a ama in kha rung phakhan, ienieinak hai kha rekhe pe. 
[22] Khania amanek a aratdet akhat na ava a ama kha vak hum phakhan, ama na irangamna pui mieriem hai kha chawi pe a shoniei hai kha mi dieng vak shem pe. 
[23] Kiei le rachang boi ama kha kiei le re`er inih, khan kiei na kiroshong pui boi ama kha na chaiachai a atet. 
[24] "Raikho rono kha na miring `khat ah put ah rushok phakhan, ama na shawmuchumna rang, ram hona mun hai kha rakan a rushe. Inia rumuk boi phakhan ruti, 'Kavakshokna ki`in khan kiei kir rang kanih.' 
[25] Ama na rukir phakhan, makha iruphietviek, khan rietienir a irutet kha vak muk. 
[26] Khashole ama na rushe a ashiedet ratha shiri shik vak chawi. Khan amani na rulut ua kha mun khan ruawm uh. Khan dawngkhit a miringpa awmchan kha matiengnek khan rushiedet joi." 
[27] Jesu na mahi rurieilai khan, mipui kar khan nupang `khat na ra`ai a ruti, "Nang navawng naikawk le nangirumujup runu kha rahvanmuh inih!" 
[28] Inia ama na ruti, "Hinek hin a rahvanmuh miring hai kha, Pathien chawng rangai a ajui miring hai kha nih." 
[29] Khan mipui kha hawng re`ep shishit phakhan, ama na ariei ruput, "Mahi rong hi ashie rong kha inih. Mahi na jetna atawk. Jonah jetna kha niboia amani dieng peh naw nih. 
[30] Ajarla Jonah kha Nineveh miring hai rang a jetna ruchang kha ang a, Miring Shapa kha mahi rong rang hin jetna kha nih rangnih. 
[31] Chawngshatna khan thang tieng ah rengnu kha na hi rong miring haihi le dingchen ning, tia amani kha aheng rangnih. Ajarla rieliei dawngkhit ah put ah Solomon varna kha rangai ahawng inih. Khan enro, Solomonnek a aliendet miring kha hin awm joi! 
[32] Nineveh miring hai kha na mahi rong miring hai hi le chawngshatna khan dingchen ning, tia aheng rangnih. Ajarla Jonah chawngriei khan amani na mulung lekna runiei uh. Khan enro, Jonahnek a aliendet miring kha hin awm joi! 
[33] "Tute na thaomiei kha muchuk shole, rethe a ninawkle tiengkum thoi ah tet mak. Khania hawng lut miring hai na mieivar kha mukna rang, thaomieishut ah itet inih. 
[34] Mik hi pum mieivar kha inih. Mik kha tha phakhan, nang pumplang kha var rangnih. Mik kha shie phakhan, nang pumplang kha jing rangnih. 
[35] Khajarkhan nang dieng a awm mieivar kha jingna boi rang, riring roh. 
[36] Nang pumplang kha var a ajing `khatshe awm nawkatu, retelang thaomiei `khat na mieivar inepek kha ang a, nang pumplang kha mieivar kha na ship rangnih." 
[37] Khan Jesu na chawngruriei a awmlai khan, Pharisee `khat na ama kha bunek puina rang, runik. Khajarkhan ama na rulut a bunek rang rushuh joi. 
[38] Jesu na bunek mala a ukhut irushukbak boi kha rumuk phakhan, Phariseepa kha shinrudawng pe. 
[39] Inia Pumi na ama dieng ruti, "Aw Pharisee hai, nangni na hai le kheng kha vai tieng nanglap uh, inia nang shung uatu phamrai le mulungpok ashie kha shipmer a awm. 
[40] Aw amawng miring hai! Vai akonpa kha na shung kha`awm ikon nimak maw?" 
[41] Khanikengta shung a awm phamrai thaw nanginiei u hai kha numpokpek a peh u roh. Khan enro, nangni rang irikip riethieng rangnih. 
[42] "Inia Pharisee hai, nangni dieng mieriemna awm! Ajarla nangni na pudina le lengmesher, khan rohon ah anshawbo hai kha shemshawm a nangpek uh. Khan chawngshatna le Pathien numpok kha ishawn boi a nangthaw uh. Shik hai kha enda boi a hihaihi nangithaw u rang a shok. 
[43] "Pharisee hai, nangni rang mieriemna awm! Ajarla nangni na synagogue ah shutna runiei kha nangdit uh, khan rievieithawna mun hai khan mi na chubai inawthaw pui u kha nangdit uh. 
[44] Pharisee hai, danoja hai, khan thanunchan thaw hai, nangni dieng mieriemna awm! Ajarla nangni hi madang boi thankhur hai kha nangnih uh; mi hai na jet boi a makhan lawn a awm uh." 
[45] Khan danthiei `khat na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Oja, hihaihi nangriei phakhan, kieini hiawm maimawk nanginepek u inih." 
[46] Khan ama na ruti, "Danthiei hai nangni diengawm mieriemna awm! Ajarla mi hai dieng iruput ngam boi pokrik kha nangpek uh. Nangni na pokrik hai kha nang khutlawi hai kar a akhatshe le shih u makche. 
[47] "Nangni dieng mieriemna awm! Ajarla nangpa hai na iruthat u thiempulien hai thanmun kha nangkon pe uh. 
[48] Diktak a nangpa hai na iruthaw chon hai kha idik, tia kungnangchang uh. Ajarla amani na thiempulien hai kha iruthat`het u inih, khan nangni na amani thanmun hai kha nangkon pe uh. 
[49] Khajarkhan Pathien na ama varna khanawm ruti, 'Kiei na amani dieng thiempulien hai le tirtawn hai kha tir rang kanih. Amani eshen kha ruthat u rangnih, khan epshawna rupek u rangnih.' 
[50] Khajarkhan makha rieliei phun ah put ah rulong thiempulien kholok thishen kha mahi rong dieng hin biena rang a inih. 
[51] Makha Abel thishen ah put ah Zachariah thishenshe nih rangnih. Zachariah na lungkheng le riethiengmun kar ah ruthi. Inih, mahi rong dieng hin a bie rang a inih, tia nangni dieng kiriei inih. 
[52] "Danthiei hai, nangni dieng mieriemna awm! Ajarla shingna chabi kha nangni na nangthe uh. Nangnirilikawm lut u makche, khan alut hai kha nangkhar uh." 
[53] Ama na hihaihi amani dieng irurieisho le roroia, danoja hai le Pharisee hai na ama kha ajeng a hawng raha pui ua, rahamothoina chawng tampui kha ama dieng rawdawn ruput u joi. 
[54] Khan ama kha shurna rang, ama na iriei rang a chawng ih khan nita`awm hengna rang, ruva a ruawm u joi."
