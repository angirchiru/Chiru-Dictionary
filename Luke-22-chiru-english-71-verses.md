# Luke 22 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 13 Nov 2026

Khan Rakanna iti Chawjawboi Rohoi kha hawng nai joi. Thiempu ulien hai le danoja hai na Jesu kha thatna rang, muruk a lam rutawk u joi, ajarla amani na miring hai kha ruchih uh. Khashole Iscariot iti Judas shung khan Satan hawng lut joi.

Rakanna kengam thatna rang a Chawjawboi Tan Nikho kha hawng shok joi. Khan Jesu na Peter le John kha rutir a ruti, "Rakanna kha nashakna u rang, she u tana vak rasham u roh."

Khan amani dieng ruti, "Mahi nangni dieng a peksha kupum kha inih. Kiei hi mininghepna rang, mahi thaw u roh."

Khan Pumi na ruti, "Simon, Simon! Satan na nangni kha gehu ang a mashap rang nadit u joi."

Ama na vak shok a eshena dungjuia Olive Lawn ah ruhawng. Ama na ruti, "Aw kapa, nang ningmuruk kha nitjoiatu, hai hi kiei ah put ah noloi pe roh."

Khan ama kha rushur u phakhan, amani na rushe pui ua, thiempu rashang in khan ruchawi u joi. Khan Peter na ralaviei a anu rujui.

Khan kholok na rurawdawn uh, "Khanitala nang Pathien Shapa kha maw nangnih?" Khan ama na ruti, "Kiei kanih, iti kha nangnitak na nangti u joi."
