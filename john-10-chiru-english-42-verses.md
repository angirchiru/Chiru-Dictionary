"Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, tunitawm kengam roko mawkawt ah hawng lut boi, inia shik khat tieng ah hawng ka, ama kha murushah le minieisho miring kha inih. Khania mawkawt ah hawng lut miring kha kengamroi kha inih. Mawkawt rungpa kha na ama rang mawlawng pe, khan kengam hai kha na ama hesho kha rangai pe uh."

Mahi chawngmeloi hi Jesu na ruriei, inia amani dieng imaw iriei, iti kha rujet u mak. Khajarkhan Jesu na amani dieng rurieiret, "Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, kiei hi kengam hai rang a mawkawt kha kanih. Kamatieng a ava kholok kha murushah le minieisho hai kha nih uh, inia kengam hai kha na amani chawng hai kha rurangai pe u mak."

Murushah kha na murukna rang le thatna rang, khan mathangna rang niboia, va mak. Khania amani na ringna kha rutukthep a mukna rang a ajong kanih. "Kiei Kengamroi atha kha kanih. Kengamroi atha kha na kengam hai rang iringkho ipek inih. Inia kengamroi nih boi, moroi miring kha na kengam hai kha atatak nit boi jarkhan, melang vat phakhan, kengam hai kha mathan a atan."

"Kiei Kengamroi atha kha kanih. Kekengam hai kha kejet, khan kiei hi amani na nejet uh. Kapa na inejettak hi ang a, kapa kha kejet, khan kengam hai rang khan kiringkho kepek. Hi roko shung a awm boi kengam hai shik kienieilai. Amani kha awm kivakchawingir rang a inih. Khan amani na kehesho kha rangai u rangnih. Khajarkhan kengam hawi khat le kengamroi khat awm rangnih."

Kapa le kiei hi khatte kanih uh. Khajarkhan Jihudi hai na ama kha lung le dengna rang, lung rushar u ret joi. Jesu na amani dieng rumashang, "Kapa ah put ah chon atha tampui kunumumuk u joi. Khahai chon kar hin ih ang kha jar a maw lung le ninedengna u rang a?"

Jihudi hai na mashang a ama dieng ruti uh, "Chon atha jar a kinedeng u rang a nimak, inia mieshietna jar a inih. Ajarla miring khat nangnih, inia nang nining a Pathien nangrasha."

Jesu na amani dieng rumashang, "Nang Dan ua irumiejiek boi maw nih? 'Kiei na kuruti, "Pathien hai kha nangnih uh." ' Pathien chawng jongna miring hai dieng pathien hai, tia la (Miejiek kha koi ngam naw nih), Apa na iruriethiengsha le manmieshie shung a inojongtir, kiei na 'Pathien Shapa kanih,' kiti jar a, 'Pathien nangmieshiet,' niniti u maw nih?"

John na akhat changna a baptisma pek a ruawmna mun, Jordan vara tieng takthar a rushe a makhan ruawm joi. Mi tampui na ama dieng va ua ruti uh, "John na jetna iawm ruthaw mak, inia John na mahi miring jar a iruriei rikip kha adik inih." Makha mun khan mi tampui na ama kha takarushawn uh.
