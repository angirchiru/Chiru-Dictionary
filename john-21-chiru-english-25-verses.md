Simon Peter na amani dieng ruti, "Ngashur she rang kanih." Ama dieng ruti uh, "Kieiniawm nang le she u rang kanih." Relentak a rushe ua rokong shung khan rulut u joi. Kha jan khan khatshe rushur u mak.

Jingkar hin Jesu na kawmchang khan ruding, nitjoiawm khaichawi hai na Jesu keng, iti rujet u mak. Khajarkhan Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Naite hai, nekleshak nanginiei boi u maw nih?" Makha dungjuia amani na ruvawr uh. Khan nga hai kha rawphai jarkhan, akaishe rumawtawr u lai mak. Jesu na numipok khaichawi kha na Peter dieng ruti, "Mahi Pumi inih!" Simon Peter na Pumi inih, iti kha rujet phakhan, kotai ruko a nining a thampui shung khan ruchawm joi.

Kawmchang ah eke rumuchum u phakhan, kha mun khan rawhaw ra ajin chung ah nga le khan tan vak muk uh. Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Tun a nangishur u nga hai kha eshen vak chawi u roh." Simon Peter na hawng ka a nga alien rajakhat shawm rahngalieithum shipmer len kha kawmchang ah hawng pawt joi.

Adi changna khan rieiret, "Jonah shapa Simon, kiei hi numnangnopok maw?" Ama dieng rumashang, "Awh, Pumi, numkonopok, iti kha nangjet." ama dieng ruriei, "Kekengam hai hi noroi pe roh."
