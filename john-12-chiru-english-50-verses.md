# John 12 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 14 Nov 2026

Rakanna rang ni ruk mala khan, Jesu na Bethany ah rushe. Makha ama na athi ah put ah irumothoi, Lazarus ruawmna kha inih. Kha mun khan amani na bunekna khat runiei pui uh. Khan Lazarus kha ama le tebol ah buneh hai kar a akhat runih. Khan Martha na chon ruthaw pe uh.

Khashole Mary na nard riethienghiei manrawp rieipartui litre khoidawr hi ruloi a, Jesu ke khan runawt pe. Khan ama ke kha asham le runo pe. Rieipartui manamhoi kha na in kha rujeibieng. Inia akhaichawi hai kar a akhat, ama ajor rang a, Simon shapa Judas Iscariot na ruti, "Shara hai dieng pekna rang, ijar a mahi rieipartui hi dinari rajathum a jorthiei boi maw nih?"

Mahi shara hai kha ire a iruti nimak, inia ama kha murushah runih. Khan shierkawk ah iruthong hai kha ama rang ruloi jawnjai a ruawm inih. Jesu na ruti, "Ama kha bieh u naw roh, kurukhumna nikho rang a itet nih joi. Ajarla shara hai kha nangawm pui jawnjai uh, inia kiei tu nawawm pui jawnjai u makche."

Jihudi mipui hawi khat na ama kha makhan awm, iti rujet u phakhan, Jesu ranga kha niboia, ama na athi ah put ah irumothoi Lazarus kha awm aen rushe uh. Khan thiempu ulien hai na Lazarus kha awm thatna rang, chawng rurashak u joi.

Jing tieng hin rohoi ah ajong mipui alientak khat na Jesu kha Jerusalem ah va joi, iti kha rujet. Naw bak hai kha rurak ua ama rumukna pui rang, rushe uh; khan amani na rura ai uh, "Hosanna! 'PUMI riming a ajong ama kha rahvantha inih!' Ama hi Israel hai Reng kha inih!"

Jesu na ranngam acha remet khat rumuk a, ama chung ah rushuh. Ajarla hitihin rumiejiek, "Chih naw roh, Aw Zion shanu. Enro, nang Rengpa kha ranngam acha remet chung ah shuh a jong joi."

Arapa khan akhaichawi hai na rujet u mak, inia Jesu kha rumulur phakhan, irumiejiek hai kha ama chungchanga inih, khan ama dieng iruthaw inih, iti kha rumininghep uh.

Khan rohoi a chubaibawk ruhawng hai kar khan Greek haiawm ruawm uh. Galilee ah awm Bethsaida miring Philip dieng va ua ruti uh, "Oja, Jesu kha kumuk jawt uh." Philip na Andrew dieng she a ruriei, khan takthar a Andrew le Philip na Jesu dieng ruriei uh.

Jesu na mashang a amani dieng ruti, "Miring Shapa lurna rang a pha kha jong joi. Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, gehu khat na pieipak ah cho a thi nawkatu, ama kha khatte a awm, inia ithi a tu, ara tampui moshok."

Jesu na ruti, "Tun karatha hi shaw joi, khan imaw kiti rang? 'Kapa, mahi pha ah put ah nashan roh.' Inia mahi rentuk rang hin a hi pha hin a kiei ajong kanih. Kapa, nang riming kha mulur rang roh." Khashole ravan ah put ah he khat jong a ruti, "Kumulur joi, khan mulurret rang kanih."

Jesu na mashang a amani dieng ruti, "Hesho hi kiei rang a jong nimak, inia nangni rangbak a ajong inih. Manmieshie chawngshatna kha tun a pha hi nih joi. Tun manmieshie rampopa kha nawt rangnih joi. Khan kiei hi rieliei ah put ah nalawm phakhan, mi kholok kha kiei dieng pawt rang kanih."

Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Mieivar hi nangni le shawtbawt awm rangnih. Ajing na nubukna boi rang, mieivar kha nangniei u lai hin lawn u roh. Khan ajing a lawn miring kha na ih ah lawn maw nih, iti jet mak. Mieivar kha nangniei u lai hin mieivar naite hai kha nangchangna u rang, mieivar kha takashawn u roh."

Mahi Isaiah thiempulien chawng kha hawng rupumna rang a inih. Ama na iruriei khala: "Pumi, nawchawngriei u khan, tu na maw takarushawn joi? Khan tu dieng maw PUMI banbo kha rophong joi?" Khajarkhan amani na takarushawn ngam u mak, ajarla Isaiah na rurieiret.

Jesu na ra ai a ruti, "Kiei hi takanashawn miring kha kiei hi taka inawshawn nimak, inia nojongtirpa kha taka ishawn inih. Khan kiei namuh miring kha na nojongtirpa kha imuk inih. Kiei hi takanashawn mi rikip na ajing a awmna boi rang, manmieshie mieivar khat a ajong kanih."
