Nicodemus iti Pharisee miring khat ruawm. Ama kha Jihudi hai lamkai khat runih. Jan a Jesu dieng ava a ruti, "Rabbi, Pathien ah put ah ajong Oja khat nangnih, iti kejet uh."

Jesu na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Diktak a nang dieng kiriei inih, tute na athar a shok joi nawkatu, Pathien rengchangna kha muh ngam naw nih."

Nicodemus na ama dieng ruti, "Miring khat na atar phakhan, itia maw shok ngam nih? Unu vawn shung ah lutthep a shok ngam nih maw?"

Jesu na rumashang, "Diktak a nang dieng kiriei inih, tukhat na tui le Ratha ah put ah shok joi nawkatu, Pathien rengchangna khan lut ngam naw nih. Taksha ah put ah ashok kha taksha inih, khan Ratha ah put ah ashok kha ratha inih."

Nicodemus na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Hihaihi itia maw nithiei nih?" Jesu na mashang a ama dieng ruti, "Nang Israel oja khat nangnih, inia hihaihi nangijet boi maw nih?"

Ramthim ah Moses na moro irulawm kha ang a, Miring Shapa kha awm ilawmngir rang a inih. Khajarkhan tunita awm ama takashawn kha na thangna niei naw nih, inia kumshawt ringna kha muk rangnih.

Pathien na manmieshie hi hitihin numrupok jarkhan, ashapa khatte kha irupek inih. Makha tunita awm ama takashawn kha na thangna niei boi a kumshawt ringna kha mukna rang a inih. Ajarla Pathien na ashapa kha manmieshie chung a chawngshat rang a ijongtir nimak, inia ama jar a manmieshie na ringna kha muknathiei rang a inih.

Chawngshatna kha hi inih, makhala manmieshie a mieivar kha jong joi. Inia miring hai na ajing kha mieivarnek khan numrupok uh.

Hihaihi shole, Jesu na akhaichawi hai le Judea ram tieng rushe joi. John awm Salim dieng ah awm Aenon khan baptisma pek a ruawm.

Amani na John dieng va ua ruti uh, "Rabbi, Jordan vara ah nang le ruawm a kungnichangnapa kha na, enro, baptisma rupek a mi kholok na ama dieng rushe u joi."

John na mashang a ruti, "Ravan ah put ah ama dieng ipek niboia, tute na iawm muh ngam mak. Moinu anieipa kha moipa kha inih. Ama kha rashang shishitngir rangnih, inia kiei tu kerede shishitngir rangnih."

Apa na ashapa kha numpok a irikip kha ama khut ah rupek joi. Ashapa takashawn mi kha na kumshawt ringna kha ieniei, khan ashapa chawng ajui boi mi kha na ringna kha muh naw nih.
