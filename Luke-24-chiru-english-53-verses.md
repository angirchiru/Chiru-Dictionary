Khan hapta shung a akhat changna nikho jingkara hin, amani na irasham u rieipartui hai kha ruchawi ua, thanmun khan rushe uh. Amani na lung kha than ah put ah rurechen kha vak muk uh.

Nupang hai kha rurawthawk ua, pieipak tieng rubu uh, khan ravantir di kha na amani dieng ruti, "Ijar a nangni na aring miring kha athi miring hai kar a nangitawk u maw nih? Ama kha hin awmlai mak, inia ruthoi joi!"

Khan amani na than ah put ah rukir ua, khaichawi shawmlieikhat dieng le lawpsheng u dieng thang vak koi u joi.

Khan enro, kha nikhotak khan miring pasha di na Emmaus iti kho khat ah khomijin rushe uh. Amani na roiruriei u lai khan, Jesu amatak na amani kha vak ranai pui a amani le rusheshom joi.

Khan amani na lam a ashok chon le ama na tan irukhoi a ihawngjetna u awmchan kha amani dieng ruriei uh.

Amani na hihaihi ruriei u lai khan, Jesu na amani kar a ruding a amani dieng ruti, "Lungdamna kha nangni dieng a awm nih rosho."

Khashole ama na amani kha Bethanyshe kha rushe pui, khan ukhut hai kha alawm a amani kha rahvan rupek. Amani kha rahvan rupeklai khan, Jesu na rurakai a amani ah put ah ravan a ruchawi joi.

Khan amani na ama kha chubairubawk ua, ningai le roroia Jerusalem ah rukir u joi. Khan amani na riethiengmun khan ningthingkip Pathien mapak a ruawm uh. Amen.
