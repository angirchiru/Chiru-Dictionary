# John 2 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 14 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 747+ verses

Nithum changna khan Galilee ah awm Cana ah moiloina khat ruawm. Makhan Jesu nuawm ruawm. Jesu le akhaichawi haiawm moiloina khan rukoi uh. Ju hawng ravat phakhan, Jesu unu na ama dieng ruti, "Ju niei u lai mak."

Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Nupang, kiei le nang na nithaw rang imaw awm? Kapha kha jong shok maklai." Unu kha na shepding hai dieng ruti, "Ama na nangni dieng itiiti kha thaw u roh."

Jihudi hai riethiengna dungjuia litre shawm shirilieirahnga ninawkle rajakhat shawmlieirahnga akeng lung baibe ruk rumiding uh. Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Baibe hai kha tui miship u roh." Khan amani na khahaikha rumishipmer uh.

Shepulien na ju rachangsha tui kha tuidan ruen phakhan, khoshekawt ah maw nih, iti rujet mak. Khan shepulien kha na moipa kha rukoi. Khan ama dieng ruti, "Mi rikip na mashapek a ju athadet eshem, inia ju athadet kha tunshe nangtetlai."

Jesu na akhat changna ama jetna kha Galilee ah awm Cana ah ruthaw. Khan ama lurna kha rumalar a akhaichawi hai kha na ama kha takarushawn uh.

Khashole unu, anaipang hai, khan akhaichawi hai le Capernaum ah rushe uh. Makhan nikho ashawt ruawm u mak.

Jihudi hai Rakanna kha hawng nai le roroia, Jesu na Jerusalem ah ruhawng joi. Riethiengmun roko shung khan shiecha hai, kengam hai, pharphali ajor miring hai, khan shierleshier rotho miring hai rushuh u kha vak muk.

Ama na ruiro le jemna rang rukon a riethiengmun roko ah put ah amani kholok kha runawt. Pharphali ajor miring hai dieng ruti, "Hihaihi hin ah put ah shok pui u roh, kapa in hi rievieithawna in machang u naw roh."

Khashole Jihudi hai na mashang a ama dieng ruti uh, "Hihaihi nangthaw jar a kieini dieng jetna imaw numumuk ngam u rawng?" Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Mahi riethiengmun hi shiet u roh, khan ni thum shung a kiei na mothoi rang kanih."

Inia makha ama pum riethiengmun jar a irurieina inih. Ama na athi ah put ah hawng thoi shole, iruriei kha akhaichawi hai na rumininghep uh.

Rakanna rohoi shung a ama na Jerusalem ah ruawmlai khan, ama na jetna iruthaw hai kha rumuk u jarkhan, mi tampui na ama riming kha takarushawn uh.
