Jesu na hihaihi ruriei shole, akhaichawi hai le Kidron tuihawr kha rakan a vara khat ah rushe joi. Makhan rohon khat ruawm. Ama na kha mun khan akhaichawi hai le rulut. Khan ama rujor Judas na awm makha mun kha rujet, ajarla makha Jesu na akhaichawi hai le ruruchun jawnjaina mun inih.

Khajarkhan Judas na thiempu ulien hai le Pharisee hai ah put ah rarup khat le riethiengmun shipai kha vak chawi a mieisher le thaomiei, khan mieriem raklam a kha mun khan va joi. Khajarkhan Jesu na khahai kholok kha ama chung a ashok rang a inih, iti kha rujet. Khan amani kha rujawng a ruti, "Tumaw nangitawk uh?"

Ama dieng rumashang uh, "Nazareth Jesu." Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Kiei kanih." Ama rujor Judas na amani le ruding a ruawm. Khajarkhan amani dieng "Kiei kanih," ruti phakhan, anuk tieng rurakai ua pieipak chung ah rupur uh.

Khashole rarup hai, rabawpkhat lamkai, khan Jihudi hai riethiengmun shipai hai kha na Jesu kha shur ua rukhop u joi. Rapapek a Annas dieng ruchawi uh, ajarla ama kha, kha kum a thiempu rashang ruchang, Caiaphas upu runih.

Khan Simon Peter le khaichawi shik khat na Jesu anu kha rujui uh. Makha khaichawi kha thiempu rashang kha le rurejet, khan ama na Jesu le thiempu rashang tolawng ah rulut u joi. Inia Peter na mawkawt vai khan ruding a ruawm.

Khashole, thiempu rashang kha na akhaichawi hai le ama iruchukna chungchanga Jesu dieng rurawdawn. Jesu na ama dieng rumashang, "Kiei na manmieshie dieng malangsheng a kiruriei inih. Pha rikip a synagogue hai le Jihudi hai na lawp a vak ruchun jawnjaina u riethiengmun khan kiruruchuk inih."

Ama na hihaihi rurieisho phakhan, kha dieng ah ruding riethiengmun shipai khat na Jesu kha ruben a ruti, "Thiempu rashang dieng khatikhan nangimashang maw nih?" Jesu na ama dieng rumashang, "Ashie kha kiruriei nitjoiatu, ashie chungchanga kungchang roh."

Khan Simon Peter na ruding a miei ru u a ruawm. Khajarkhan ama dieng ruti uh, "Nangawm akhaichawi hai kar a akhat nimakche maw?" Ama na rurapha a ruti, "Kiei nimi ing." Peter na onokor ituk pe inko, thiempu rashang shok hai kar a akhat na ruti, "Rohon ah ama le awm kinurumuk boi maw nih?" Peter na makhan rurapharet, makhan arkhawng hawng khongrelen joi.

Jingkara hin Jesu kha Caiaphas ah put ah Praetorium khan amani na ruchawi u joi. Khan rono boi a Rakanna shakna u rang, amani na Praetorium khan rulut u mak. Khajarkhan Pilate na amani dieng vak shok a ruti, "Hi miring chung hin ih nawngna maw nivakchawi uh?"

Amani na mashang a ama dieng ruti uh, "Chon shie ruthaw joi nawkatu, ama hi nang dieng nepeh u naw ning." Pilate na amani dieng ruti, "Ama kha chawi u tana nang dan u dungjuia ama chung a chawngvakshat u roh." Jihudi hai na ama dieng ruti uh, "Miring khat that rang a hi kieini rang dan nimak."

Khashole Pilate na Praetorium khan rulutret, khan Jesu kha rukoi a ama dieng ruti, "Nang Jihudi hai Reng kha maw nangnih?" Jesu na ama dieng rumashang, "Mahi nang nining a nangiti maw nih, ninawkle miring shik hai na nang dieng kajar a iruti maw nih?"

Pilate na rumashang, "Kiei hi Jihudi maw kanih? Nang phung miring hai le thiempu ulien hairilik na nang kha kiei dieng inepek u inih. Imaw nangiruthaw awm joi?" Jesu na rumashang, "Kerengchangna kha tu manmieshie ah put ah nimak. Kerengchangna kha mahi manmieshie ah put ah nitjoiatu, koshok hai na Jihudi hai dieng kerepekna boi rang, ra thaw u rangnih."

Pilate na ama dieng ruti, "Khanitala reng maw nangnih?" Jesu na rumashang, "Reng nangnih, nangiti kha keng. Ajarla adik khan kungkachangna rang, manmieshie a jong shok kanih. Adik a awm mi rikip na kawchawng kha rangai."

Pilate na ama dieng ruti, "Adik kha imaw nih?" Khan hihaihi ruriei shole, Jihudi hai dieng rusheret a amani dieng ruti, "Ama chung a shietna khatshe muk pe mi ing. Rakanna nikho a miring khat kimoshok rang a hi nangni rang shena dan inih. Khajarkhan nangni rang Jihudi hai Reng kimoshok rang a hi nangdit u maw?"

Kha pha khan amani kholok na ra airet a ruti uh, "Hi miring hi nimak, inia Barabbas kha!" Barabbas kha minieisho miring runih.
