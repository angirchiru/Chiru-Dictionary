Khan Mary le uliennu Martha awmna kho Bethany khan Lazarus iti miring khat ana a ruawm. Pumi kha rieipartui irunawt a eke hai kha asham le iruno pe ama kha mahi Mary hi inih. Anaipangpa Lazarus kha runa pe. Khajarkhan uliennu hai kha na Jesu dieng chawng mawchawi a ruti uh, "Pumi, enro, numnangipok ama kha ana a awm joi."

Rujet phakhan, Jesu na ruti, "Mahi natna hi thitnathiei nimak, inia Pathien lurna rang a inih, khan mahi jar hin Pathien Shapa kha mulurna rang a inih." Jesu na Martha, anaipangnu, khan Lazarus kha numrupok. Khajarkhan, ana kha rujet joiawm, ruawmna mun khan ni di ruawmlai.

Makha shole khaichawi hai dieng ruti, "Judea ah she u ret rawi." Akhaichawi hai na ama dieng ruti uh, "Rabbi, Jihudi hai na tuntun hin nang kha lung le deng rang, natawk uh. Khawn sheret rang a nangiti maw nih?" Jesu na rumashang, "Nikho khat a nidan shawmlieidi awm nimak maw?"

Ama na hihaihi ruriei shole, amani dieng ruti, "Nawlawm u Lazarus kha miei joi, inia meche rang, kiei she rang kanih." Akhaichawi hai na ruti uh, "Pumi, amiei nitatu atha rang keng." Khashole Jesu na amani dieng madanghet a ruriei pe joi, "Lazarus kha thi joi."

Jesu na vat phakhan, Lazarus na than shung ah ni mili ruawm kha vak jet joi. Bethany kha Jerusalem ah put ah majin di dawr inih. Anaipangpa u chungchanga Mary le Martha mawai rang, Jihudi hai tampui vak ruchun uh.

Martha na Jesu dieng ruti, "Pumi, hin nangruawm a la, kanaipangpa khaw ruthi naw nih. Khania tunawm Pathien dieng nanginik kha Pathien na nang dieng nepek rangnih, iti hi kejet." Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Nang naipangpa khaw thoiret rang keng."

Jesu na ama dieng ruriei, "Kiei hi thoiretna le ringna kha kanih. Kiei hi takanashawn miring hai kha na thit joiawm ring rangnih. Khan tunitjoiawm kiei dieng a aring a takanashawn mi rikip na kumshawt a thitna kha niei naw nih. Mahi takanangshawn maw?" Ama dieng ruti, "Awh, kupumi, kiei na nang hi manmieshie a ajong Christa, Pathien Shapa kha nangnih, iti hi takakawshawn joi."

Mary na thoi a rawnawttak a rushe kha, in ah awm ama mawai Jihudi hai kha na rumuk u phakhan, "Thanmun ah achap she keng," tia ama kha rujui uh. Jesu ruawmna mun khan Mary na rushe a ama kha vak muk phakhan, ekekung a rubawklap a ama dieng ruti, "Pumi, hin nangruawm a la, kanaipangpa khaw ruthi naw nih."

Khajarkhan Jesu na ama achap le ivatpui Jihudi hai achap kha rumuk phakhan, ajeng a mulung rushit pe a rushaw pui. Ama na ruti, "Ama kha khoshekawt ah maw nangreh uh?" Ama dieng ruti uh, "Pumi, va tana vak en roh." Jesu na ruchap.

Khashole Jesu na mulung runat puiret jarkhan, than khan rushe joi. Makha koh runih, khan makha lung le rumangai uh. Jesu na ruti, "Lung hi melet u roh." Athipa uliennu Martha na ama dieng ruti, "Pumi, manam shie rang keng joi, ajarla ni mili nih joi." Jesu na ama dieng ruti, "Takanangshawn joiatu, Pathien lurna kha nangmuk rangnih, tia kinururiei boi maw nih?"

Khajarkhan athipa rehna lung mawkawt kha rumelet uh. Khan Jesu na chung tieng ruen a ruti, "Kapa nangnururangai pe jarkhan kanamapak. Nangnarangai pe jawnjai, iti hi kejet."

Hihaihi ruriei shole, he rashangtak a rura ai, "Lazarus, vai vak shok roh." Ukhut le ke hai kha ponramasha le rokhop, khan amai kha ponte le rejemlam a athipa kha vai vak shok joi. Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Ama kha thut u tana mawlawn u roh."

Khajarkhan Mary dieng ava Jihudi hai kar a mi tampui na Jesu na ithaw hai kha rumuk u le, ama kha takarushawn uh. Inia eshen na Pharisee hai dieng rushe ua Jesu na iruthaw hai kha vak riei uh.

Amani hai kar a akhat, kha kum a thiempu rashang, Caiaphas na amani dieng ruti, "Nangni iawm jet u makche. Miningawm mining u makche, makhala phung kholok athang rangnek a miring khat vanoi ruhut a athi kha athadet inih."

Khajarkhan khani ah put ah ama kha itia maw that rang, tia chawng rurashak u joi. Khajarkhan Jesu na Jihudi hai kar a malangsheng a rulawnlai mak. Inia ramthim dieng ah awm ram khat, Ephraim iti kho khan rushe joi.

Khan Jihudi hai Rakanna kha hawng nai joi, khan mi tampui na kha ram ah put ah Rakanna mala a nining ua riethiengna rang, Jerusalem ah ruhawng uh.
