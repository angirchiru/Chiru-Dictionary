Khashole Jesu na Galilee Thampui vara ah awm Tiberias Thampui tieng ruhawng joi. Ama na ana hai dieng a iruthaw jetna hai kha rumuk u jarkhan, mirawp tampui na anu rujui u joi.

Jihudi hai rohoi, Rakanna kha hawng nai joi. Jesu na iringrakang a en phakhan, mirawp tampui na ama tieng ava kha rumuk a Philip dieng ruti, "Amani shak rang khoshekawt a tan vak rawchawk rang a maw nih?"

Philip na ama dieng rumashang, "Dinari rajadi man tan hi na amani dieng mum khatshe a awm chang u naw nih."

Mahin nim tan rahnga le nga di aniei pashanai khat awm, inia mi hijat rang hin imaw ang nih? Jesu na ruti, "Miring hai kha mushut u roh." Kha mun khan champi tampui ruawm. Irumushut u miring hai kha bawp rahnga dawr runih.

Khajarkhan Jesu na tan hai kha ruloi, khan mapakna runiei a khaichawi hai dieng rupek. Amani kha vawn miship pe u phakhan, akhaichawi hai dieng ruti, "Lekvai mienieng hai kha khatshe thangna boi rang, rawkhawm u roh."

Khashole rawkhawm ua, aneh-ashah hai kha na imak u nim tan rahnga lekvai hai kha tiengkum shawmlieidi rumiship uh.

Kho hawng romo phakhan, akhaichawi hai na thampui tieng jong chum u joi. Rithi ranaktak a hawng rang jarkhan, thampui kha rurawnawk. Amani na majin thum ninawkle mili dawr rujap u le, Jesu na thampui chung ah vak lawn kha rumuk uh.

Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Kiei kanih keng, chih u naw roh." Khashole ama kha rokong shung ah rumulut u joi.

Jing tieng hin thampui vara khat a arangah mipui kha na akhaichawi hai rulutna kha niboia, rokongte shik rumuh u mak. Makhan mipui na Jesu awm, khan akhaichawi haiawm rumuk boi u phakhan, rokongte hai khan rulut ua Capernaum ah Jesu atawk va u joi.

Amani na thampui vara khat tieng ama kha rumuk u phakhan, ama dieng ruti uh, "Rabbi, hi mun hin itik a ava maw nangnih?"

Jesu na mashang a amani dieng ruti, "Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, jetna kha nangrumuk u jar a ninatawk u nimak, inia tan kha nangshak ua vawn nangmiship u jar a inih. Athangthiei nekleshak rang khan chon thaw u naw roh, inia kumshawt ringnashe a a awm rang a, Miring Shapa na nangni dieng inepek rang a, nekleshak rang khan chon thaw u roh."

Khajarkhan ama dieng ruti uh, "Pathien chon hai kha thawna rang, imaw kithawthiei u awm?" Jesu na mashang a amani dieng ruriei, "Ijongtir ama kha takanangishawn u kha Pathien chon inih."

Khashole Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, Moses na ravan ah put ah tan kha nangni dieng nurupek u mak, inia kapa na ravan ah put ah tan adik kha nangni dieng nepek uh."

Jesu na amani dieng ruriei, "Kiei hi ringna tan kha kanih. Tunitjoiawm kiei dieng a ava ama kha na itikte nita awm vawncham pe naw nih, khan tunitjoiawm kiei takanashawn ama kha na itikte nita awm tuira pe naw nih."

Khashole Jihudi hai na ama kha mieshiet ruput uh. Ajarla ama na ruriei, "Kiei hi ravan ah put ah ajong tan kha kanih." Amani na ruti uh, "Mahi Jesu hi Joseph shapa, unulepa kijet u kha nimak maw?"

Khashole Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Diktak a nangni dieng kiriei inih, Miring Shapa taksha kha nangshak u nawk a, khan ama thishen kha nangnek u nawkatu, nangni na ringna kha niei u makche. Kataksha kha ashah, khan kithishen kha aneh mi kha na kumshawt ringna kha ieniei."

Hihaihi Capernaum ah awm synagogue khat ah ruruchuklai khan a iruriei inih.

Akhaichawi hai tampui na rurangai u phakhan, ruti uh, "Mahi chawng matat inih. Tu na maw jet ngam nih?"

Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Hi na inakhar u maw nih? Miring Shapa kha na matieng a ruawmna mun ah aka kha nangmuk uala, imaw ti u rawng? Ringna apeh kha Ratha inih."

Hijarhin akhaichawi hai tampui na ama ah put ah rurakai ua ama anu rujui u lai mak. Khajarkhan Jesu na shawmlieidi dieng ruti, "Nangniawm nangshe jawt u joi maw?"

Simon Peter na rumashang, "Pumi, kieini na tu dieng maw she u ning? Nang na tu kumshawt ringna chawng hai kha nangniei. Nang hi Christa, aring Pathien Shapa kha nangnih, iti hi takakawshawn u joi, khan kejet u joi."
