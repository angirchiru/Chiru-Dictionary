Hishole Jihudi hai na Jesu kha that rang rutawk u jarkhan, Judea khan ruleng jawtlai mak. Khajarkhan ama na Galilee shung khan riephiei a ruawm joi. Khan Jihudi hai Thamber hai rohoi kha hawng nai joi.

Khajarkhan anaipang hai na ama dieng ruti uh, "Nang khaichawi hai na nangithaw hai kha muknathiei rang, hi mathan tana Judea tieng she rang roh. Ajarla vanoi na ijet atawk miring tute na rethe a chon thawngai mak."

Jesu na amani dieng ruti, "Kapha kha jong shok maklai, inia nang pha u kha tu rasham jawnjai a awm. Manmieshie na nangni inumukmak u rang a kha anithiei boi inih, inia kiei tu numukmak uh. Rohoi khawn nangni she u roh. Makhaw rohoi khawn kiei tu she naw ninglai, ajarla kapha kha hawng rupum maklai."

Inia anaipang hai kha rushe shole, malangsheng a niboia, muruk a ama awm rohoi khan rushe joi. Rohoi mun khan Jihudi hai na rutawk ua ruti uh, "Ama kha ih ah maw awm?"

Inia rohoi laitak khan Jesu na riethiengmun khan ruhawng a mi ruruchuk. Jihudi hai na shindawnglam a ruti uh, "Hi miring hi na behang ruchuh boipet a, itia miejiek hai hi ijet maw nih?"

Jesu na mashang a amani dieng ruti, "Kiei na kiruchuk hi katah nimak, inia nojongtirpa tah inih. Moses na nangni dieng Dan kha inurupek boi u maw nih? Khania tute na Dan kha rung u makche. Ijar a kiei hi thatna rang ninatawk u maw nih?"

Khan Jerusalem miring eshen na ruti uh, "Ama hi nimak maw amani na that rang a irutawk u kha? Khania, enro! Malangsheng a ama na chawngriei, inia ama dieng iawm ti u mak."

Khashole Jesu na riethiengmun ah put ah miruchuklam a ra ai a ruti, "Kiei hi nangnejet uh, khan khoshekawt ah put ah maw kanih, iti nangjet uh. Kinining a ajong nimi ing, inia nojongtirpa kha adik inih, ama kha jet u makchelai."

Nikengta mipui kar a tampui na ama kha takarushawn ua ruti uh, "Christa na jong phakhan, hi miring hi na iruthaw jetnanek hin arawpdet jetna kha jong thaw nih maw?"

Makhan Jesu na ruti, "Nangni le pha bawngkhat awm rang kanilai, khashole nojongtirpa dieng ahawng rang kanih joi. Nangnatawk u rangnih, inia numuh u naw tinih. Kaw awmna khan va ngam u naw tinih."

Rohoi nikho dawng alientak khan Jesu na ruding a ra ailam a ruti, "Tunitjoiawm tuira miring kha na kiei dieng va rieshie tui kha neh rosho. Kiei takanashawn miring kha na, Miejiek ah iruriei kha ang a, mulung ah put ah tuiring vadung hai kha long pe rangnih."

Mahi takashawn miring hai na imuk rang a rasham Ratha chungchanga iruriei inih. Ajarla Jesu kha rumulur boilai jarkhan, Ratha Riethieng kha rupeh maklai.

Mipui kar a tampui na chawng kha rurangai u phakhan, ruti uh, "Diktak a ama hi Thiempulien khat inih." Shik miring hai na ruti uh, "Ama hi Christa kha inih." Shik miring hai na ruti uh, "Galilee ah put ah Christa shok nih maw?"

Eshen na ama kha shurna rang, rumining uh, inia tute na ama chung a khut rubang ngam u mak.

Khashole riethiengmun shipai hai na thiempu ulien hai le Pharisee hai dieng rushe uh. Khan amani dieng rurawdawn uh, "Ijar a nangishur boi u maw nih?" Riethiengmun shipai hai na rumashang uh, "Miring tute na awm hi miring hi ang a chawngruriei maklai!"

Amani kar a akhat a ruawm, Jesu dieng jan a rushe, Nicodemus na amani dieng ruti, "Nadan u na chawng rangai mala le ithaw kha imaw nih, iti jet mala, chawng maw shat?"

Ama dieng mashang a ruti uh, "Nangawm Galilee ah put ah maw nangnih? Tawk tana enro, Galilee ah put ah thiempulien thoi maklai."

Khan mi rikip na i inin ah rushesho u joi.
