# Luke 16 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 13 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 280+ verses total now!

Khan Jesu na akhaichawi hai diengawm ruriei pe, "Rawchawng miring khat ruawm, ama na inchonpo khat runiei. Ienieinak hai kha pawrshawnawk a thaw, tia ama chung a chawng hawng tung joi."

"Inchonpo kha na nining a ruti, 'Kiei na imaw kithawthiei awm? Kupumi na inchonpona kha kiei ah put noloi pe rangnih joi."

Dikna boi inchonpo kha na shingveng a ruthaw jarkhan, pumi kha na rumapak. Khan kiei na nangni dieng kiriei inih, dikna boi nieilelung kha le lawm thaw u roh.

Rawchawng miring khat na ponshenrin le ponramasha thaning rieshiei a ruawm. Ama na ningthingkip lungtawr a nekleshak rushak. Khan Lazarus iti shara miring khat apan a tuisharlam a apakawt khan ruawm.
