# Luke 23 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 13 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 618+ verses!

Mipui kha na ruthoi ua Jesu kha Pilate dieng ruchawi u joi. Khan amani na ama kha mieshiet ruput ua ruti uh, "Mahi miringpa hi na nuphung u hi pawrmechet joi."

Khan Pilate na ama kha rawdawn a ruti, "Nang Jihudi hai Reng kha maw nangnih?" Khan ama na rumashang, "Nangiti kha inih."

Khan amani na iruchawi u le roroia, Simon iti Cyrene miring khat, cross kha rangrat a rumuphung ua, Jesu anuk a rumawlawn uh.

Khan amani na Calvary iti mun kha rushok u phakhan, ama kha cross ah rumakhai u joi. Khan Jesu na ruti, "Kapa, amani dieng hin rangaidam peh roh, ajarla amani na ithaw u hi ijet boi u inih."

Khan nidan shawmlieidi ah put ah nidan thum dawrshe hin ajing kha na rieliei kholok jong mukhu joi.

Khan Joseph iti lamkai miring khat ruawm. Ama kha mitha le midik khat runih. Ama na Pathien rengchangna kha ngak a ruawm inih.
