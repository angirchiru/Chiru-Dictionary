# Luke 21 - Chiru - SS Samungir Chiru - 13 Nov 2026
## Chiru First Digital Corpus - 491+ verses!

Khashole Jesu na khongrunga a rawchawng miring hai na nieibawmna khan amani khutchawi hai iruthong u kha rumuk. Khan mieirawng shara khat na lepton di iruthong kha awm ama na rumuk. Khan ama na ruti, "Kiei na nangni dieng diktak a kiriei inih, mahi mieirawng shara hi na amani kholoknek a arawpdet ithong inih."

Khan riethiengmun kha idawr a metha lung hai kha le khan khutchawi hai kha le irielien maw nih, tia mi eshen na ruriei u phakhan, ama na ruti, "Nangimuk u hihai nieinak hai rang hin nikho jong rangnih, makha pha khan akhat chung ah akhat rotho achim boi a imak lung khatshe awm naw nih."

Khan ama na ruti, "Nangni kha nomohonna boi u rang, riring u roh. Phung khat na phung khat chung a ra thaw rangnih, rengchangna khat na rengchangna khat chung a ra thaw rangnih."

Khan Miring Shapa kha ranak le lurna alientak khat le romiei chung ah ajong kha amani na muk u rangnih.
