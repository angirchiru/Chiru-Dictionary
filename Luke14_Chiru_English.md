# Luke 14 - Chiru (First Digital Corpus) - SS Samungir Chiru

## Chiru Version

Khan hitihin ruawm, Sabbath nikho khat hin Jesu na Pharisee hai lamkai khat in ah buneh rushe phakhan, amani na ama kha tinruta uh. Khan enro, pomrai tawng miring khat amaton a ruawm. Jesu na mashang a danthiei hai le Pharisee hai dieng rurawdawn, "Sabbath nikho a mi madam hi Dan maw nih, ninawkle dan nih boi maw nih?" Inia amani ruawmchien uh. Khan Jesu na ama kha apawttap a rumadam a rumeshe joi. Khan ama na amani dieng ruti, "Nang kar ua nang shapa khat ninawkle nang shiecha khat hi Sabbath nikho khan khur ah cho joiala, relentak a roshoh naw rawng maw?" Hihai chung hin amani na rumashang ngam u mak.

Khan koimiring hai kha na runiei a richit ipek itawk u awmchan kha rumuk le, amani dieng rangshim khat ruriei pe: "Tukhat na nangni kha moiloi bumichinna khan nokoi u phakhan, shutna hoi hai khan shuh u naw roh. Miringpa kha na nangnek a richit pekdet rang a miring koithiei. Nang dinre akoipa kha na 'Nang mun kha hipa dieng hin peh roh,' tia nang dieng nitithiei, khan jaklam a, nang na renemving shutna kha nanglothiei."

## English NKJV Parallel - Luke 14:1-10
[1] Now it happened, as He went into the house of one of the rulers of the Pharisees to eat bread on the Sabbath, that they watched Him closely.
[2] And behold, there was a certain man before Him who had dropsy.
[3] And Jesus, answering, spoke to the lawyers and Pharisees, saying, "Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?"
[4] But they kept silent. And He took him and healed him, and let him go.
[5] Then He answered them, saying, "Which of you, having a donkey or an ox that has fallen into a pit, will not immediately pull him out on the Sabbath day?"
